# NXTPTT Support

NXTPTT is a push-to-talk walkie-talkie: hold to talk, let go to listen.

## Getting started

1. Open NXTPTT and enter the name others will see.
2. Pair with someone: on the **Contacts** tab, both tap **+ → Optical handshake** and hold the phones
   screen to screen, tops together, about 15–20 cm apart. Not together? Send your link with
   **+ → Share my link**.
3. On **Talk**, hold the orb and speak. Let go to listen.

## Talk groups

**Channels → +** (or **Contacts → + → New talk group**) creates a group; members are optional. Tap the QR button on a group to invite people: they scan it with **Contacts → + → Join a group**
and are added once your phone receives their request.

## Common questions

**They don't hear me when their phone is locked.** Both phones need notifications allowed
for NXTPTT (Settings → Notifications) and the latest version.

**Messages arrive late.** When a direct connection isn't possible, messages go through the
encrypted iCloud relay, which depends on iCloud and network conditions. Being signed in to
iCloud helps.

**The optical handshake doesn't finish.** Hold the phones tops together about 15–20 cm apart,
tilt a little if there's glare, and keep them still until both show the same six-digit code.

**Replay button is grey.** The talker didn't allow replay for that message, or it's more than
an hour old.
