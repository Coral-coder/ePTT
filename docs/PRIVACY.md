# NXTPTT Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 September 2026_

NXTPTT is a push-to-talk walkie-talkie for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It is built so that
we, the developers, never receive your voice, your messages or information about you.

## What we collect

Nothing. NXTPTT has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no
servers run by us.

## What stays on your device

- Your name as you entered it, your encryption keys, your contacts and channels.
- The list of recent transmissions shown in Activity.
- Microphone audio is captured only while you hold the talk button. Camera images, used to
  scan talk group codes and for the optical handshake, are processed on the device and never stored
  or sent.

## What goes to the people you talk to

When you pair with someone, your devices exchange your chosen name, public keys, network
addresses and push-notification tokens, so that they can reach you. This goes only to the
people you pair with and the talk groups you join.

Voice and control messages are end-to-end encrypted. Every transmission uses a fresh key that
only the intended recipients' devices can open.

## Services NXTPTT uses to deliver messages

- **Direct connections.** Nearby devices talk over peer-to-peer Wi-Fi and Bluetooth; others
  connect over the internet, device to device.
- **Address lookup (STUN).** To find a direct route, the app asks public STUN servers run by
  Google (`stun.l.google.com`) and Cloudflare (`stun.cloudflare.com`) for your device's public
  internet address. Those services see your IP address for that request, under their own
  privacy policies. You can turn this off in Settings.
- **Apple Push Notifications.** Used to wake a recipient's device and deliver encrypted call
  alerts. Apple sees that a notification was sent to a device, not its content.
- **iCloud relay.** If a recipient can't be reached directly, the encrypted message is left in
  NXTPTT's iCloud (CloudKit) container, under a mailbox name that changes daily. Nobody,
  including us and Apple, can read it. It is deleted once picked up, and in any case after
  24 hours. You can turn the relay off in Settings.

## Replays

The person who talks decides whether a message may be replayed. Recipients can replay only the
last message they received, and only for one hour. That copy is kept only on their device,
protected by iOS file encryption, and is replaced by the next message or discarded after the
hour.

## Children

NXTPTT is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from
anyone.

## Your choices

Remove a contact or leave a group at any time. Deleting the app deletes everything it
stored on your device. Messages waiting in the iCloud relay expire within 24 hours.
